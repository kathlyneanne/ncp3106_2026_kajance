01 / COMPUTER ENGINEERING

COMPUTER ENGINEERING

Is a four-year engineering program that embodies the science and technology of design, development, implementation, maintenance, and integration of software and hardware components in modern computing systems and computer-controlled equipment.

02 / UE CPE

UE COMPUTER ENGINEERING

The UE Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering degree program inspires learners to zero in on the design, development, and integration of computer systems through combined principles across electrical engineering and computer science. Computer Engineering Warriors get to do deep dives into the study of hardware components such as digital circuits, microprocessors, and embedded systems, as well as software development and system architecture and much more.

Computer Engineering connects physical computing devices with the software that controls them. A project might use sensors and circuits for input, a microcontroller for processing, and code to decide how the system responds.
