01 / DEFINITION

Computer Engineering is the field that connects computer hardware and software to create complete, intelligent, and reliable computing systems.

Computer Engineers work with electronic circuits, processors, embedded systems, programming, computer networks, and other technologies. They make physical computing devices and software work together efficiently.
